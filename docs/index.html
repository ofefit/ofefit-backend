About OfeFit

OfeFit is a community fitness group in Cape Coast, Ghana.

We move together: outdoors, at the university and in local venues. Fitness works better when it is shared, so every session is built to bring people together.

Move together

Weekly classes from Monday to Saturday, plus a monthly sunrise walk and our signature events.

Eat well

Members get a simple weekly meal plan built around protein, vegetables and local favourites.

Stay connected

Our WhatsApp tribe shares the schedule, routes and event details as they are confirmed.
